About

Hi, I'm Aitor

I'm a final-year Computer Engineering and Data Science & AI student at the University of Deusto, applying machine learning to energy systems, optimization, and quantitative problems. My work spans academic research at DeustoTech — three co-authored papers on building energy forecasting and demand-side management — and an applied GenAI internship at McKinsey QuantumBlack.

Outside of research and coursework, I care about ideas that hold up outside the notebook: interpretable models, honest evaluation, and results that still make sense on real data. I'm social by nature, competitive in sports, and enjoy building things end to end.
